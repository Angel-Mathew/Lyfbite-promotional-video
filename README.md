# Lyfbite Promo Video

Lyfbite is an online business idea that my group created for a university project. We had to come up with a business and build a prototype of it. This is the promo video we made for it.

## The promo

The video is about 11 seconds long. It shows plates of fresh food around the line **"Eat better. Live better. Simple meals, weight loss."** and ends with the Lyfbite name.

We wanted it to be short and friendly, because a promo has only a few seconds to explain what the business is about.
